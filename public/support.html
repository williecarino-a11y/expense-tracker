Frequently Asked Questions

How do I update my default currency?

Navigate to Settings and select Currency Settings to update your preferred display currency instantly.

Are my financial records securely encrypted?

Yes, all data synced to MongoDB Atlas and transaction logs are handled with end-to-end security protocols.

How do I export my transaction data?

Visit the Data Export page to download your complete logs in either CSV or JSON format.
